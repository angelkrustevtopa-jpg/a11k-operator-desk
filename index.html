Engagement principles

A useful system begins with a bounded problem.

Human ownership

The operator remains accountable for pricing, approvals, communications, and final commercial judgment.

Evidence first

Recommendations should point back to supplied portfolio context and clearly labelled research inputs.

Narrow before broad

We begin with one commercial workflow that can be reviewed, measured, and improved.

Privacy by design

Share only the data required for the agreed pilot; sensitive information stays under the operator's control.
